Bring your comics into Coeia. Follow dialogue in another language, lose yourself in the artwork, and keep the moments that stay with you.

01

LET THE STORY MOVE FORWARD

Stop switching between your comic and a translation tool. Coeia places translated text on the original page, keeping dialogue beside the characters and you inside the story. Switch back to the original whenever you want to compare.

02

A PLACE FOR YOUR FAVORITES

Gather your comics into a personal library, organize them in folders, and pick up where you left off. Import PDF, CBZ, CBR, ZIP, RAR, and other supported formats. Upload over Wi-Fi or connect your own SMB, WebDAV, and OPDS sources.

03

OPEN A COMIC. SETTLE IN.

Turn pages smoothly and browse thumbnails to find the panel you want to revisit. Smart two-page mode pairs adjacent portrait pages while letting landscape spreads stand on their own. Find your comfortable way into the story.

04

KEEP MORE THAN WHOLE BOOKS

An expression, a composition, a panel that makes you pause: turn it into a card. Choose a shape, create still or animated effects, and collect, share, or display it in a Home Screen widget. Bring a little of your favorite stories into everyday life.

Export original comic files and completed translations for backup or to keep reading elsewhere.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Coeia is a reading tool and does not provide comics. Please import your own files.

Comics, translations, and cards are stored on this device. Content does not sync through the cloud; export backups to keep it safe.

Translation is a paid feature. Results vary with image clarity, lettering, and backgrounds. Try a few pages first to see whether it suits your comics before continuing.
