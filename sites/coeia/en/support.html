Frequently asked questions

How do I import my comics?

Gather your comics into a personal library, organize them in folders, and pick up where you left off. Import PDF, CBZ, CBR, ZIP, RAR, and other supported formats. Upload over Wi-Fi or connect your own SMB, WebDAV, and OPDS sources. Coeia is a reading tool and does not provide comics. Please import your own files.

What should I know before translating?

Translation is a paid feature. Results vary with image clarity, lettering, and backgrounds. Try a few pages first to see whether it suits your comics before continuing.

Do my comics sync to another device?

Comics, translations, and cards are stored on this device. Content does not sync through the cloud; export backups to keep it safe.

Can I export my comics and translations?

Export original comic files and completed translations for backup or to keep reading elsewhere.

What can I do with cards?

An expression, a composition, a panel that makes you pause: turn it into a card. Choose a shape, create still or animated effects, and collect, share, or display it in a Home Screen widget. Bring a little of your favorite stories into everyday life.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: “Me → Account settings → Delete account”. There is a 30-day cooling-off period, and signing in during it cancels the request. You can also read this guide:

Account Deletion Guide
