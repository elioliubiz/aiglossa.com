Label guide

Ingredients, translations, and allergens

See how ingredient order, original wording, translations, inferred notes, and allergen cautions should be read.

The list has an order

Ingredients are generally listed from the largest to the smallest amount by weight. This gives context, but it does not reveal an exact amount unless the package states one.

For imported labels, keeping the original term beside a translation makes the explanation easier to verify.

Facts and inference are different

An allergen explicitly printed on the package is different from a possible concern inferred from an ingredient name. AIGLOSSA marks inferred information instead of presenting it as label text.

If the photo is unclear or a detail is absent, check the physical package before making an allergy-related decision.
