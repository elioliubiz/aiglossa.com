Label guide

How to read a nutrition label

Understand per 100 g, per serving, NRV, sodium, and salt without turning the table into a health score.

Start with the basis

Check whether values are listed per 100 g, per 100 ml, or per serving. A serving is chosen by the manufacturer and may not match the amount you eat.

Use the same basis when comparing two products. Per 100 g or 100 ml is usually the most direct comparison.

Read percentages in context

NRV or daily-reference percentages show how much a labeled amount contributes to a general adult reference. They are not a health score and do not describe your personal needs.

Sodium and salt are related but not identical labels. The report preserves the unit and wording shown on the package.
