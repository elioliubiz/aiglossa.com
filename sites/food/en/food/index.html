Before you scan

What should be visible in the photo?

Upload one clear photo per scan. Frame the ingredient list or nutrition table you want the report to read.

Can it read imported or foreign-language labels?

Yes. The report keeps relevant original wording and adds a localized explanation when the text is readable.

What if the nutrition table is missing?

The report says the value is unavailable. It does not treat missing information as zero or invent a number.

Is NRV a health score?

No. NRV is a general daily reference used to put a labeled amount in context, not a rating of the food.
