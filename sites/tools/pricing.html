How credits work

  • 30s track = 45 credits, 45s = 64, 60s = 85, 75s = 103, 90s = 120.
  • Failed generations are refunded automatically.
  • Credits never expire and work across all tools.
  • Payments are processed by Creem, our merchant of record.
  • Unused credits can be refunded within 14 days — see the Refund Policy.
